    <h2>Research</h2>
    <p class="muted">My current work is organized around four connected themes:</p>
    <ul style="margin:8px 0 0 18px;">
      <li><strong>Orbital and valley transport</strong> — generation, dynamics, accumulation, and detection of non-spin angular-momentum degrees of freedom.</li>
      <li><strong>Moiré and supermoiré quantum materials</strong> — reconstructed bands, magnetic Bloch states, Hofstadter physics, and interaction-driven phases.</li>
      <li><strong>Quantum geometry and nonequilibrium responses</strong> — Berry curvature, orbital moments, symmetry, and geometric contributions to transport and optical response.</li>
      <li><strong>Atomic many-body physics and neutral-atom quantum science</strong> — high-precision atomic structure, Rydberg interactions, and theory for neutral-atom platforms.</li>
    </ul>
